Why High Predictive Accuracy Fails Without Explanation Reliability in Clinical AI

Over 85% of clinical XAI studies rely on anecdotal visual inspection. Under routine ICU bedside sensor noise (h = 0.05), deep tabular neural networks experience up to 9.6× higher attribution instability and >40% cross-explainer disagreement across SHAP, LIME, and Integrated Gradients. C-XAI introduces a 5-layer quantitative auditing platform establishing that calibrated tree ensembles deliver +19.6% higher net clinical decision utility.
